Dokhona VTON
1. Open dokhona-tryon.html in Chrome or Edge (internet needed for body and hand tracking).
   If the camera is blocked, run "python -m http.server" in this folder and open http://localhost:8000/dokhona-tryon.html
2. Camera: stand back so your full body shows. Make a fist near a yellow point to grab it, move your hand, open your palm to release.
3. Make new dresses with dokhona-creator.html (upload a transparent PNG, place the 10 points, Save to folder).
   Each dress is a folder: <name>/dress.png + <name>/points.json
4. In the try-on, click "Load dress folder" and choose the "dresses" folder to add them.
